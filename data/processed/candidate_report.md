## Does it join to the German posting data?

**Barely.** 7 of 73 candidate skills have an equivalent in our German extraction (10%), and those appear in only **3.3%** of German IT postings.

Shared: CI/CD, Docker, Java, JavaScript, Power BI, Python, SQL

Missing from the candidate vocabulary but common in German IT demand: AI/ML, Embedded, Data Science, Data Engineering, Security, Cloud, SAP, Network.

> Consequence: treat this as a **standalone supply-side fixture**. Matching it against
> German postings needs a skill-vocabulary bridge that does not exist yet.

## The shipped 'ground truth' does not mean what it looks like

- Labelled pairs: 75,000 (30 per opening, always the same size)
- Share satisfying the documented rule: **100.0%** — so the labels are internally consistent...
- ...but the qualified pool averages **866 candidates**, of which the labels cover **3.5%**.
- Labels sharing the opening's seniority: **33.6%** (random baseline ~33%) — seniority is ignored entirely.
- Labels sharing the opening's role title: 33.2% (random baseline ~4%) — role does carry signal.

> **Do not report retrieval precision against these labels.** They are an arbitrary
> top-30 slice of a much larger qualified set, so unlabelled correct answers are
> everywhere and any honest matcher will look wrong.
